FitBharat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. The food you log, your weight, water, workouts and profile (age, height, weight) are stored only on your own device, inside the app. We do not run a server and we do not have an account system.

आप जो खाना, वज़न, पानी, वर्कआउट और प्रोफ़ाइल की जानकारी डालते हैं, वह सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में रहती है। हम इसे अपने पास नहीं भेजते।

Your data and backups

You can export your data as text from the Profile tab and keep it yourself. If you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, the data on the device is deleted.

प्रोफ़ाइल टैब से आप अपना डेटा टेक्स्ट के रूप में निकाल सकते हैं। ऐप हटाने या स्टोरेज साफ़ करने पर फ़ोन का डेटा मिट जाता है।

Third parties

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google may receive your device's IP address for that request. If we add ads or analytics in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change goes live.

Health notice

FitBharat gives general information. Calorie values are approximate. It is not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are under 18, talk to a doctor or a dietitian before changing your diet or exercise.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
